    Texts

    Every inscription is a real ancient text: the Rosetta Stone (British Museum EA 24), the harpers’ songs, the offering formula, epithets of Hathor, Alcaeus fr. 332, 346, 366 and 333, the Seikilos epitaph, Sappho fr. 2 and 31, Greek tourist graffiti from the Valley of the Kings, and Papyrus Chester Beatty I. Greek is quoted from the standard editions. Hieroglyphs marked ◇ are my own sign-by-sign typesetting of attested words. They can differ from a specific original monument, so check them against a sign list before you cite them. The English translations are mine and follow the standard scholarly ones.
